# Aset Ayyashop

## Banner

File proyek: public/images/hero.webp. Dibuat menggunakan tool ImageGen bawaan, kemudian dikompresi ke WebP untuk website. Tidak memakai CLI atau API key eksternal.

Prompt final:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photorealistic e-commerce homepage hero background for an Indonesian family online store named Ayyashop. Create a premium, softly sunlit studio still-life on a continuous very light powder blue backdrop. Wide landscape composition 1536x1024: arrange all products in the RIGHT HALF only, leaving the LEFT 48 percent empty pale powder blue, with no text or graphics at all. Subjects: an elegant oatmeal canvas tote bag with caramel leather handles standing on a pale blue plinth, a pair of clean white-and-navy lifestyle sneakers in front, a small sage green ceramic vase with a single leafy branch, and two minimal warm cream skincare bottles. These are generic unbranded objects, beautifully proportioned, realistic fabric weave and leather details, art directed retail photography, subtle long soft shadows and tasteful blue tonal round pedestal. Camera slightly above eye level, high-end product campaign feel, pale sky blue and cream with touches of caramel, soft natural daylight. Products fill right side and lower right, with generous breathing room. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark. The left half must remain empty for HTML overlay headings. The image will be cropped to a wide website banner.

## Font dan ikon

- Plus Jakarta Sans: [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans). Font variabel disimpan lokal di public/fonts/jakarta.ttf. Lisensi SIL Open Font License ada di public/fonts/OFL.txt.
- Ikon antarmuka: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), dependensi lucide-react, lisensi ISC.
- Wordmark, favicon, serta ikon placeholder Ayyashop dibuat sebagai SVG di dalam proyek.
